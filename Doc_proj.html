Netflix Visualization Project

Design Process

I explored the Netflix Titles dataset, which includes metadata for over 8,000 titles. My goal was to visually answer key questions such as: When was the most content released? Which genres dominate specific countries? And how do movies and shows differ in duration?

After sketching initial ideas, I built several visualizations using D3.js — including a scatterplot, bar chart, and line chart — while continuously refining filters and styling based on the insights I found.

Design Rationale

  • Scatterplot: Duration vs. release year, colored by type and sized by cast size.
  • Bar Chart: Top genre-country combinations show regional content patterns.
  • Line Chart: Trends of release counts over time to reflect Netflix growth.
  • Dropdown Filters: Allow filtering by content type and rating for targeted insights.

Insights & Findings

  • Netflix’s biggest release surge occurred from 2017 to 2019.
  • Genres like “Drama” and “International Movies” appear most often across regions.
  • TV shows consistently use “1 Season” as duration, estimated as 60 minutes in the visual.
